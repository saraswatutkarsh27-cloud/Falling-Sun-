About

Falling Sun is a two-day under-18 hackathon where students build real, working prototypes. No hollow slide decks and no vaporware. Physics engines in Godot, real-time websites in React, or sensor circuits on an ESP32, we want things that actually run.

Solo or up to 4 membersAll skill levels welcomeBring your own hardware

Three tracks

Game Development

Build something playable: mechanics, procedural generation, physics or atmosphere. Godot, Unity, Unreal, Phaser, Raylib and more.

Web Development

Build real web applications that work under pressure, from real-time apps to full-stack tools.

Robotics

Microcontrollers, sensors and servos. Build a machine that does something in the physical world.

Schedule

Day 1 · First 12 hours

The build window. Get the problem statements, set up your workstation and commit your first code. Mentors review your design and hardware pinouts. Midpoint repository snapshot, then overnight rest.

Day 2 · Final 12 hours

Polish and showcase. Push features, fix bugs and rehearse your demo. Hard code freeze, then stage pitches to the judges, followed by awards.

Exact dates and timings will be announced on WhatsApp.

Prizes and sponsors will be revealed soon. Updates go out on the official WhatsApp channel.

FAQ

What is Falling Sun?

An under-18 hackathon where young builders spend 2 days (12 hours + 12 hours) making real projects in Game Development, Web Development and Robotics.

Can I join solo or in a team?

Both. Teams can have up to 4 members.

I have never been to a hackathon. Should I still join?

Yes. All skill levels are welcome and mentors will be around.

What should I bring?

Your laptop and any hardware you need. Bring your own hardware for the robotics track.

When are the dates and prizes announced?

On the official WhatsApp channel, as soon as they are confirmed.
